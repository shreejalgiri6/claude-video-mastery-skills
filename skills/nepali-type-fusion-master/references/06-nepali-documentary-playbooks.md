# Nepal-Specific Documentary Storyboards and Styling

## Index
Audience ethics, migration stories, fraudulent work ads, mental health, financial information, NRNA/civic, helplines, scenario patterns.

## Audience and ethics

The priority is respectful Nepali public information, not entertainment at the expense of viewers affected by exploitation, death, anxiety, or fraud. Cinematic storytelling is an editing language; avoid sensational disaster aesthetics, stigma, or implying fabricated events. Do not replace numbers, quotes, warning texts, institution names or contact numbers without verification/permission.

## 1. Overseas work / airport departure

**Tone:** aspiration + distance + uncertainty, not automatic tragic music.

**Visual plan:** distant airport environment or abstract travel imagery if visuals allowed; documentary type on top. Establish a geographical direction and carry motion into next beat. If no footage available, typography alone can signal departure with controlled slide and negative space.

**Text hero example:** `विदेशिन्छन्` may appear as large off-white word with a restrained red thin treatment if user explicitly wanted danger coding. Never turn each word into a comic flight takeoff preset.

**Fusion:** footage/background with very slight horizontal drift; headline Text+/PNG on separate compositing plane, transform at meaningful vocal pivot; B-roll has slower relative movement to convey depth. When user wants no footage/icons, animate type only.

## 2. Scam / fake Europe recruitment

**Tone:** cautionary investigative explainer. No jump-scare horror, fake cash shower, or deceptive interface branded as real proof.

**Narrative system:** Hopeful promise → online claim → inconsistency → actual risk → verified action. Highlight direct warning terms (`पर्यटक भिसामा`, `ठगी`, `जोखिम`) in red only when appropriate; unverified monetary figures must remain neutral or be withheld.

**Scene construction:** first phrase gentle and confident; warning arrives by meaningful hard cut or controlled lateral shift; red does not glow dramatically. Document screenshots must be legitimate or clearly fictionalized, not presented as real.

**SFX:** one precise subtle reveal sound, no loop of cheap swipes. The title should still tell a coherent story muted.

## 3. Mental health of overseas workers

**Tone:** empathetic, calm, hopeful, never stigmatizing. Prefer isolated space / abstract environmental imagery when no faces desired. No alarming red flashing behind suicide mentions. Don't romanticize death or provide graphic visual metaphor.

**Typography:** warm white on soft dark background; single hero phrase with full readable hold. Use quiet and steady pace, perhaps no SFX. For closing words e.g. `मन दुख्दा लुकाउने होइन, सुनाउने हो।`, avoid bouncing punctuation or spinning text. A neutral centered statement often beats kinetic shock effects.

**Phone numbers:** all digits and institutions must be sourced or supplied verbatim. Hold long enough to note down. Numbers should never be animated as rapidly cycling counters. Check Nepali vs international digit grouping and outside/Nepal country calling context.

## 4. Savings, bond, social security or migrant-worker finance

**Tone:** clear and trustworthy. When introducing `वैदेशिक रोजगार बचत पत्र`, hero term is clean, large; secondary label explains what it means in plain Nepali. Financial stats should include source, time period and qualifier if supplied.

**Data reveals:** number weight + unit + comparison to avoid ambiguous metrics. Keep `४० लाख` separate from misleading approximations; don't silently add claims. A numeric ticker must show only meaningful known values or be explicitly abstract: do not simulate false precision.

**Actions:** 2D editorial slide for mechanism; line of text for explanatory concept; mild 2.5D parallax for contextual image. No shiny animated gold coins unless explicitly wanted and context calls for them.

## 5. NRNA and civic information

**Tone:** formal but energetic. Establish institutional identity only from verified supplied assets. Use full organization name on first appearance where needed. Captions remain readable and aligned.

Title intro examples: `NRNA 10th September 2026` (if explicitly provided) may use exact text, however if user requires Nepali-only display, offer Nepali rendering with authorization. Do not assume official branding colors or logos from memory.

## 6. Good vs poor NGO animation

| Poor | Better |
|---|---|
| Every beat has a yellow box | Yellow used only for verified action/data emphasis |
| Whoosh on every text move | Sound only on semantically significant cut |
| Suicide statistic shakes violently | Calm, legible and sourced presentation |
| 3D text spin on a helpline | Stable text with clear contact information |
| Random zoom from same point for every word | Consistent camera direction with strategic cadence changes |
| Decorative icons added to text-only scene | Thoughtful size/weight/whitespace hierarchy |
| Number rolling through invented intermediate values | Rendered discrete states or verified counting methodology |

## 7. Scenario checklist

- Distinguish creative reenactment from genuine archival/actual footage if potentially confusing.
- Distinguish data on-screen from the source's date and the claim's time range.
- Do not use manipulative emotional cues in trauma messaging.
- Verify official contact and helpline information if not directly supplied, especially with recent dates.
- Keep hotline/CTA visible, high contrast, silent readability; no quick disappearing numbers.
- Keep user-requested absence of faces and extra imagery.
- If script is a fixed recording, retain text verbatim and time to the actual sound rather than redesigning dialogue.
