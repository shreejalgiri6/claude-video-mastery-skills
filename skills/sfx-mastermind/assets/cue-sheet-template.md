## Editorial QA

- [ ] Complete spoken text and critical numbers remain understandable.
- [ ] Silent/no-effect decisions are intentional.
- [ ] No suspicious implied reality or fabricated evidence.
- [ ] Source material/perspective match visuals.
- [ ] No repetitive whoosh/glitch/impact signature.
- [ ] No audible pops/clicks/clipped peaks or cut tails.
- [ ] Mono/small speaker tested when possible.
- [ ] Attribution rights and license records retained.
- [ ] No assets marked auditioned, downloaded, or mixed without actual tool action.
